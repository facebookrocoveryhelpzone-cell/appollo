Principles

Four rules that keep every screen feeling like Wellora.

White first

Pages open on a clean white canvas. Mint gradients add light behind heroes and at section edges, and stay out from behind body text.

One emerald

Emerald is the only saturated brand color. Warning and critical colors are separate and appear only when something needs attention.

Big, bold type

Headlines are large, heavy and tightly spaced. Body text stays calm, with generous line height and short lines.

Soft edges

Rounded corners, hairline borders tinted green, and low, wide shadows. Nothing in the system has a hard black outline.

Patterns

Page rhythm for the Wellora landing page.

Backgrounds alternate between white and mint so each section reads as its own moment. Two dark or saturated bands mark the emotional peaks.

Navigationpill bar · --bg
Hero with phonemint wash · trust row overlaps edge

  • Width. Content is 1,200 px at most, with a 16 px minimum gutter on phones.
  • Section padding. 96 px top and bottom on desktop, 56 px on phones.
  • Grids. Cards sit three across on desktop and stack to one column on phones.
  • Headings. One eyebrow, one headline and an optional lead, always left aligned.
  • Imagery. Phone mockups on a mint glow, and natural light photography with rounded corners.
  • Motion. Gentle fades and 3 px card lifts only. Everything respects reduced motion.
  • Health claims. Show the line that Wellora is a wellness companion, not medical advice, near any health data.
